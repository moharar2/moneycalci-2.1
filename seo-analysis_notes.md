## Target public crawl: MoneyCalci
Source URL: https://moneycalc-pqpzjtta.manus.space/
Observed title: MoneyCalci — Calculate Smarter. Decide Better.
Observed homepage H1: Free calculators for smarter money decisions.
Observed information architecture: homepage, /calculators, category links for /finance, /loans, /investment, /savings, and individual calculator routes for loan, mortgage, compound interest, and savings.
Observed page strategy: calculator-first landing pages with calculator UI, result summaries, formula/explanation blocks, FAQs, and related-calculator links. The homepage also contains a search field and category cards.
Observed limitation: the deployed site exposes a small Version 1 footprint and does not provide measurable organic performance or backlink evidence by itself.

## Competitor public crawl: Calculator.net
Source URL: https://www.calculator.net/financial-calculator.html
Observed title/H1: Financial Calculators.
Observed architecture: one broad financial hub linking dozens of dedicated calculator pages grouped into mortgage/real estate, auto, investment, retirement, tax/salary, and other financial categories.
Observed strategy: high breadth and exact-match calculator URL inventory, dense internal linking, search on the hub, and category/tag-style links repeated near the page footer.
Competitive implication: Calculator.net's visible advantage is topical breadth and extensive internal-link coverage; the available crawl does not establish its traffic or ranking cause.

## Competitor public crawl: Bankrate
Source URL: https://www.bankrate.com/calculators/
Observed title: Financial Calculator | Free Online Calculators from Bankrate.com.
Observed strategy: a marketing-led calculator hub with hero content, four featured tools, and grouped clusters for mortgage, auto, credit cards, home equity, investing/CDs, personal finance, small business, college, and loans. The hub also cross-links rate comparison products and educational/commercial sections.
Competitive implication: Bankrate combines calculator intent with adjacent rate-comparison and editorial intent, creating multiple commercial pathways beyond the calculator itself.

## Competitor public crawl: NerdWallet
Source URL: https://www.nerdwallet.com/finance/calculators
Observed title: Financial Calculators - NerdWallet.
Observed strategy: category clusters for auto, banking, debt, credit cards, home/mortgage, insurance, personal finance, retirement/investing, small business, student loans, and taxes. Many links are editorially phrased and include explanatory or decision-oriented titles rather than only exact-match calculator names.
Observed trust/commercial context: global finance navigation, advertiser disclosure, sign-in/sign-up, editorial guidelines, licensing/disclosure links, and commercial product categories.
Competitive implication: NerdWallet's visible moat is breadth plus editorial/trust infrastructure and product adjacency; the crawl alone cannot prove ranking or traffic causality.
